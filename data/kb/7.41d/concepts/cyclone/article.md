# Cyclone

Cyclone is a status effect that lifts afflicted units, completely disabling them while rendering them invulnerable. Some sources also apply a basic dispel to the target. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/cyclone@2252519]

## Mechanics

Cyclone is considered a stun and disables like one while moving the affected unit upward and spinning it counter-clockwise. If the cyclone expires normally without interruption, the unit lands facing the same direction it faced when cycloned; consequently, the rotation speed is not constant and adapts to the cyclone’s duration. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/cyclone@2252519#Mechanics]

The affected unit also wobbles through the air, with its x and y coordinates changing randomly but smoothly by up to ±50. These shifts can affect abilities, including causing Dream Coil’s leash to snap or Cold Feet to be dispelled because of the distance from its marker. The exception is Windranger with the Compass of the Rising Gale equipped: she does not wobble when self-casting Eul’s Scepter of Divinity or Wind Waker. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/cyclone@2252519#Mechanics]
