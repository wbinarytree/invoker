# Spell Immunity: Enemy Interactions [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/spell_immunity_enemy_interactions@2383442]

## Interactions with Spell-Immune Enemies

Spells interact differently with spell-immune enemies: most cannot affect them, some affect them only partially, and some fully pierce spell immunity. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/spell_immunity_enemy_interactions@2383442#Interactions_with_Spell_Immune_Enemies]

## Fully Piercing

These abilities fully affect spell-immune units. True Sight always pierces spell immunity. The list was tested on a hero who was spell immune from the start; some effects, such as slows, continue working if spell immunity is obtained after their application. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/spell_immunity_enemy_interactions@2383442#Fully_piercing]

### Bounces

The following can bounce from and to spell-immune units, excluding ward-type units. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/spell_immunity_enemy_interactions@2383442#Choosing_as_target]

| Ability |
|---|
| Luna – Moon Glaives (no dmg if bounced to courier) |
| Snapfire – Buckshot |
| Storm Spirit - Overload |

They can bounce from spell-immune units including ward-type units, but can bounce to spell-immune units only when they are not ward-type. They can bounce to a courier if it was the main target, but the bounce has no damage. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/spell_immunity_enemy_interactions@2383442#Choosing_as_target]

## Partially Piercing

These abilities are neither fully effective against spell-immune enemies nor fully blocked. Usually, their main component pierces spell immunity while secondary effects are blocked. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/spell_immunity_enemy_interactions@2383442#Partially_piercing]
