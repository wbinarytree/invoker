# Neutral Buildings

Neutral buildings do not directly belong to either faction, but may be captured by or used by either faction. They are permanently invulnerable, cannot be destroyed, and are immune to every ability. Both teams can always see them through the Fog of War. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/buildings_neutral_buildings@2220325]

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Capturable buildings | Players can conquer them for their faction; the opposing faction can recapture them. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/buildings_neutral_buildings@2220325] |
| True neutral buildings | They never belong to either faction but can be used by both. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/buildings_neutral_buildings@2220325] |

## Outposts

Outposts are located within both teams’ primary jungles near the Bounty Rune spots, and beyond both safe lanes toward the map edge near the Tier 1 Towers. There are a total of 4 Outposts. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/buildings_neutral_buildings@2220325#Outposts]

Outposts initially belong to the team on their side. An enemy can capture an Outpost within a primary jungle after destroying any Tower (Tier 2). A team can retake an enemy-acquired Outpost without destroying an enemy Tower (Tier 2). The Outposts at the map edge cannot be captured. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/buildings_neutral_buildings@2220325#Outposts]

Outposts are always visible through the Fog of War regardless of alliance. Capturing one plays an animation and channeling sound that the other team can easily notice. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/buildings_neutral_buildings@2220325#Outposts]

## Twin Gates

Twin Gates are located at the north-west and south-east corners of the map near the Roshan pits. There are a total of 2 Twin Gates. Although they use Radiant and Dire visual themes, they belong to neither faction and can be used equally by all players. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/buildings_neutral_buildings@2220325#Twin_Gates]

Twin Gates are always visible through the Fog of War. Without vision over a Twin Gate, enemies cannot see when it is used. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/buildings_neutral_buildings@2220325#Twin_Gates]
