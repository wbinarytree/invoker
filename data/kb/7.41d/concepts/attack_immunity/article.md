# Attack Immunity

Attack Immunity prevents attacks from affecting a unit. Ranged instant attacks may target attack-immune units but have no effect if the unit remains attack immune on impact; instant melee attacks cannot target them. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/attack_immunity@2299977]

## Definition

| Mechanic | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Ethereal | Grants Attack Immunity and self-disarms. The unit is immune to all physical damage but usually, though not always, has reduced magic resistance. | Decrepify [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/attack_immunity@2299977#Definition] |
| Non-targetable | Does not include Hide sources. Grants Attack Immunity and Phased. The unit is not selectable, loses its Selection Box, and cannot be targeted by allies or enemies. | Hitch A Ride [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/attack_immunity@2299977#Definition] |
| Misc Non-targetable | The unit can remain selectable and retain its Selection Box but cannot be targeted by allies or enemies. | Smoke Screen [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/attack_immunity@2299977#Definition] |

Attack-immune units cannot be targeted by attacks or damaged by instant attacks. They can disjoint any attack projectiles and do not draw Aggro unless Taunt is rendered. Consequently, neutral creeps do not look for sources when receiving damage from attack-immune units. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/attack_immunity@2299977#Definition]
