# Disarm

A visual effect appears over disarmed units. Disarm prevents an afflicted unit from attacking but does not prevent ability casting. When ordered to attack, the unit attempts to stay within attack range of its target but cannot act; it resumes attacking normally when the disarm expires. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/disarm@2353573]

## Sources

Besides the listed abilities, nearly all ethereal and hex sources disarm affected units. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/disarm@2353573#Sources]

## Instant attacks

Most instant attacks follow this interaction rule: an instant attack generated outside the normal attack animation ignores disarm. If it is generated together with a normal attack animation, including various multishot instant attacks, it is not generated because the normal attack is not allowed. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/disarm@2353573#Instant_Attack]
