# Universal

Universal heroes do not have a Primary Attribute. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/universal@2372468]

## Bonuses

### All Attribute Items

Each attribute has its own set of focused items. The items are divided into five sections:

| Section | Attribute boost | All-attribute boost | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tiny Items | — | 1 | Consist only of Iron Branch. It is usually taken as a starting item to make up for gaps in a hero's stats. |
| Small Items | 3 | 2 | Usually taken as starting items to make up for gaps in a hero's stats. |
| Medium Items | 6 | 4 | Generally not taken to increase attributes; usually components of many midrange items. |
| Large Items | 10 | 6 | Usually components in more powerful items and almost never bought just for the stats. |
| Ultimate Items | 25 | 15 | Mostly used to make late-game items because they are expensive and cost-ineffective per point of stats. |
