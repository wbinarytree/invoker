# Channeling

Channeling causes the caster to stop acting for the ability’s duration. A channeled ability may end early if the channeling unit performs another action or is interrupted by another ability or disable. While an ability is being channeled, an on-screen bar indicates the time remaining. Silence stops ability channeling, while mute stops item channeling. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/channeling@2378740]

## Interrupting Channeled Abilities

The following actions interrupt ability channeling:

- Issuing another command, including move, left-clicking the ground, stop, attack, or using A on a target.
- Casting another ability or using an item, with certain exceptions.
- Receiving a disable that would normally prevent the unit from casting abilities.

If interrupted before the ability’s effects occur, the mana and cooldown are wasted and the effect is cut short or, in some cases, does not occur at all. By contrast, non-channeled abilities with high cast times, including Assassinate and Requiem of Souls, do not expend mana or enter cooldown until completed. Certain channeled abilities, including Illuminate and Powershot, still perform their effects when canceled, usually at the cost of damage.

Any disable that would normally prevent the unit from casting the spell in question also interrupts channeling, including stuns, cyclones, hexes, sleeps, and getting hidden. Although they do not prevent casting, most root effects also interrupt channeling. Silence interrupts ability channeling only; mute interrupts item channeling.

Some forced-movement abilities, including Vacuum and X Marks the Spot, interrupt channeling even when used on friendly units. Other abilities, including Force Staff and Boulder Smash, apply forced movement without causing an interrupt state and do not interrupt channeling.
