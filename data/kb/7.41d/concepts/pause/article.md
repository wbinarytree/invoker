# Pause

Pause is a deprecated status effect that never saw use in Dota 2 and is retained only for archival purposes. Spells that used Pause in DotA 1 instead apply a regular stun or prevent affected units from acting in Dota 2. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pause@2123931]

## Effects

Pause completely disables affected units, preventing them from:

- Moving or turning
- Attacking or casting spells
- Using, picking up, dropping, or selling items
- Receiving new player-issued orders during the pause; orders given or queued before it began are carried out after it ends

Paused units receive the lowest priority from enemy AI. Pause also suspends the duration of most debuffs and prevents them from dealing damage over time, except spells based on the channeling ability Aerial Shackles—the base spell of Shackles, Dismember, and Fiend's Grip—or on the channeling ability Life Drain. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pause@2123931]

Pause fully disables:

- Evasion
- True Sight
- Damage block
- Permanent Invisibility-based spells: Permanent Invisibility, Spin Web, Moonlight Shadow, Nature's Guise, and Smoke of Deceit
- Permanent Immolation-based spells: Rot, Permanent Immolation, and Permanent Immolation
- Building Damage Aura-based spells: Radiance
- Bladestorm-based spells: Blade Fury [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pause@2123931]

## Other uses

The Circle of Powers—a special immobile unit owned by each player in the fountain area that mainly serves as a stash—has a **Freeze Hero** ability. It indefinitely pauses and makes invulnerable the hero of an ally who left the game. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pause@2123931#Other_uses_of_pause]

The **-unstuck** chat command pauses the player’s hero for 60 seconds, then moves it to the player’s team fountain. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pause@2123931#Other_uses_of_pause]
