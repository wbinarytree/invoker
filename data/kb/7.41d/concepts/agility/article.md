# Agility

Agility is one of the three primary attributes. It grants every hero attack speed and armor; agility heroes also gain attack damage per point of agility. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/agility@2370050]

## Bonuses

| Per point of agility | Increase |
|---|---:|
| Base armor | 0.167 |
| Attack speed | 1 |
| Main attack damage for agility heroes | 1 |

Gaining 1 armor requires 6 agility points. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/agility@2370050#Bonuses]

## Agility heroes

Because agility heroes gain both damage and attack speed as their primary attribute increases, they tend to have the highest physical damage output as the game progresses and scale well with offensive items. They tend to have limited disables or nukes; almost all their abilities augment their physical capabilities or provide positioning or escape mechanisms. Consequently, they are generally more vulnerable and weaker early, when strong offensive disables and nukes dictate kills, but with good items they can deal the greatest late-game damage while taking the least physical damage because of their high armor. Agility heroes are divided roughly evenly between melee and ranged heroes and primarily fill carry and ganker roles. There are 35 agility heroes.
