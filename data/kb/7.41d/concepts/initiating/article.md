# Initiating

## Overview

Initiating is a hero’s capability to cripple a group of enemies before a clash, giving their team the upper hand. It is central to mid- and late-game success and concerns starting a teamfight so that one’s team is already advantaged. Without well-suited heroes, teamfights may become messy or the team may repeatedly be attacked first. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433]

## Heroes

Initiators are a primary driving force in winning team clashes. Most have one or more area-of-effect abilities that inflict heavy damage and/or severe crowd-control effects on multiple enemy heroes. Some instead damage singular targets to help eliminate them, usually the enemy Carry. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#Heroes]

Initiators generally double in the following roles: [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#Heroes]

| Support |

Playing an Initiator requires boldness and awareness of the team’s intentions; when initiation begins, the team must be able to commit. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#Heroes]

For a low-HP hard-support Initiator, the best hope of survival is not necessarily more HP but causing as much chaos as possible. The more damage or disable the hero delivers, the less likely enemies are to kill them before their allies kill those enemies. Shiva's Guard or Veil of Discord may therefore be safer than armor or HP for heroes that are poor at tanking; when uncertain, Mekansm helps both the hero and the team. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#Items]

Many Supports’ initiation combinations have long cooldowns. After landing every spell, the hero may contribute little during the remainder of the teamfight; when low on HP, they should cast their teamfight spells and items quickly, then back off. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#Items]

## The Key to Initiating

A good initiation often depends on the following ingredients: [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#The_Key_to_Initiating]

| Ingredient |
|---|
| A hero with Blink Dagger and a game-changing AoE disable |
| A warded map |
| A very good sense of intuition and map awareness |

### Approaches

Several approaches can begin a favorable fight: [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#The_Key_to_Initiating]

| Approach |
|---|
| Catch the opposing team while it is badly positioned and one’s teammates are ready to pounce |
| Isolate and immediately kill one enemy, then mop up the rest when they clump together to defend that hero |

The Initiator is effectively responsible for the team’s aggression. Some commonly labeled Initiators are also effective at immediately winning a teamfight already underway, because opponents may avoid clumping under the threat of huge AoE damage until they have an opportunity to attack. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#The_Key_to_Initiating]

| Hero |
|---|
| Sand King |
| Earthshaker |
| Enigma |

### Surprise and disabling

Initiation aims to disable as much of the enemy as possible so opponents cannot escape when the team attacks. Appearing unexpectedly in their midst makes landing an AoE disable easier because they have little time to react. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#The_Key_to_Initiating]

| Surprise method | Interaction |
|---|---|
| Blink Dagger | Often used to give enemies as little reaction time as possible |
| Shadow Blade | Can provide surprise, but can be countered by Gem of True Sight and is therefore not as good |
| Rune of Invisibility | Can provide surprise, but can be countered by Gem of True Sight and is therefore not as good |

A hero with a long-range, large-area stun may instead charge at the opponent and launch it. The stated examples are: [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#The_Key_to_Initiating]

| Hero | Ability |
|---|---|
| Tiny | Avalanche |
| Warlock | Chaotic Offering |
| Leshrac | Split Earth |

Opponents may also have stunners, and a fraction of a second’s delay can result in the Initiator being stunned while the enemy executes a successful initiation. Teamfights can be won in mere seconds, so one misstep can be costly. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#The_Key_to_Initiating]

### AoE damage

Heroes dealing high AoE damage can also be Initiators because they force enemies to scatter and, rarely, flee. A well-used Echo Slam could slash an entire team’s HP in half; enemies attempting to escape then give the Initiator’s team an opportunity to charge in and massacre them. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#The_Key_to_Initiating]

### Positioning and vision

It is best to initiate where enemies cannot spread out. A teamfight’s goal is to eliminate as many opposing heroes as possible, whereas initiating in an open area may send them in many directions, limit follow-up Nukers’ spells, and make wiping out the enemy team—the most desired teamfight outcome—more difficult. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#The_Key_to_Initiating]

Good warding is imperative. Without detailed map vision, it is difficult to know whether a lone hero is bait for a teamfight one will not initiate, where to set a trap, or whether the team can arrive in time to prepare it; the positioning of the Initiator’s backup is vital. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#The_Key_to_Initiating]

“Strike when your enemy is most vulnerable” is the basic principle of initiation: the AoE disable makes the enemy vulnerable, leaving the team to mop up the opposition. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/initiating@2143433#The_Key_to_Initiating]
