# Teleport

## Overview

Teleportation is a form of movement that instantly moves a target from one point to another. The most common teleports are Blink from Blink Dagger and Teleport from Town Portal Scroll. Teleports have varying properties and may be treated differently by different effects. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/teleport@2340208]

## Mechanics

A teleport instantly changes the affected unit’s current coordinates without travel time. Although mechanically identical, instantly moving effects are classified as follows: [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/teleport@2340208#Mechanics]

| Classification | Definition |
|---|---|
| Teleporting | Global teleportation of any kind |
| Blinking | Short-ranged teleportation affecting the casting unit |
| Reposition | Short-range forced teleportation of other units |

Most blinking and teleporting abilities may be disabled and uncastable while rooted or leashed, and they usually disjoint projectiles. These properties are inconsistent and depend on the individual ability. Some global teleporting abilities have a delay before teleportation through an effect delay, long cast time, or channeling. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/teleport@2340208#Mechanics]

### Disjointing

All blink-based abilities disjoint, but not all teleporting abilities do. Blink or teleport distance does not matter; projectiles are disjointed when the ability moves the unit. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/teleport@2340208#Disjointing]
