# Attributes

Attributes are the main statistics of every hero and independently determine most scaling statistics. The three attributes are strength, agility, and intelligence. Heroes gain attributes by leveling up, obtaining talents, buying certain items, or using certain abilities; illusions also benefit from attributes. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/attributes@2369347]

## Primary Attribute

Every hero has a primary attribute, which modifies main attack damage for each point of that attribute. It heavily influences—and commonly defines—the hero’s role and playstyle; intelligence heroes, for example, are typically more dependent on abilities. A hero’s primary attribute typically grows faster than the other two attributes. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/attributes@2369347#Primary_Attribute]

## Strength

Strength measures toughness and endurance and determines maximum health and health regeneration. Strength-primary heroes can be hard to kill and often serve as initiators and tanks, starting fights and absorbing most damage from enemy attacks. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/attributes@2369347#Strength]

## Agility

Agility measures swiftness and dexterity and determines armor and attack speed. Agility-primary heroes tend to depend on physical attacks and items while using abilities as a fallback, and they often serve as carries and gankers. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/attributes@2369347#Agility]

## Intelligence

Intelligence measures wit and wisdom and determines maximum mana, mana regeneration, and magic resistance. Intelligence-primary heroes tend to rely on abilities to deal damage or help others, and they often serve as supports, gankers, and pushers. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/attributes@2369347#Intelligence]

## Universal

Universal heroes benefit from every attribute. Although they gain less from concentrating on a single main attribute, they can choose items according to their abilities and bonuses rather than primarily for item attributes. Their item build can therefore shift them between support and carry roles without binding them to one main attribute. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/attributes@2369347#Universal]

## Attribute Bonus

Attribute Bonus is a passive self-affecting ability available to every hero except certain heroes, such as Invoker. It is located below the talent tree in the Talent leveling menu and grants all-attribute bonuses. It automatically levels when nothing else can be leveled at that hero level. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/attributes@2369347#Attribute_Bonus]

## Items Granting Attributes

Attribute-focused basic items are divided into five sections:

| Section | Attribute increase | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Tiny | Iron Branch only; all attributes by 1 | Usually a starting item used to cover gaps in a hero’s statistics. |
| Small | Associated attribute by 3, or all attributes by 2 | Usually starting items used to cover gaps in a hero’s statistics. |
| Medium | Associated attribute by 6, or all attributes by 4 | Generally purchased as components of midrange items rather than specifically for attributes. |
| Large | Associated attribute by 10, or all attributes by 6 | Usually components of more powerful items and almost never purchased solely for their statistics. |
| Ultimate | Associated attribute by 25, or all attributes by 15 | Mostly used for late-game items because they are expensive and cost-ineffective per attribute point. |
