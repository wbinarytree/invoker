# Intelligence

Intelligence is one of the three primary attributes. It grants mana, mana regeneration, and magic resistance to every hero. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/intelligence@2381953]

## Bonuses

Every intelligence point provides:

| Bonus | Per intelligence point |
|---|---:|
| Maximum mana | 12 |
| Mana regeneration | 0.05 |
| Magic resistance | 0.1% |
| Main attack damage for intelligence heroes | 1 |

The mana bonus applies only to the maximum mana pool. Current mana is not increased, but retains its current percentage. Gaining 1 mana regeneration requires 20 intelligence points. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/intelligence@2381953#Bonuses]

## Intelligence Heroes

Intelligence heroes tend to have more active abilities than other heroes. Every intelligence hero has at least three active abilities; many have four active abilities, and some have 5 or more. Their larger mana pools and higher mana regeneration help them use these abilities frequently. Collectively, they have by far the greatest number of disabling capabilities and are essential in most lineups.

Their item builds tend to be highly caster-based because those items primarily provide intelligence. Most are ranged, with a few exceptions. Most rely on spells for damage, but these usually have static damage values increased only by spell damage amplification or damage manipulation. Consequently, most peak in the early to mid game, when their spell damage is greatest relative to enemy health pools and resistance, then fall off in the late game and often fill support or ganker roles. Exceptions with scaling abilities can excel in the late game.
