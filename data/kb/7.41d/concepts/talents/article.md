# Talents

## Overview

Talents are hero-specific traits that enhance attributes, stats, and abilities. Each hero has 8 talents unlocked during a match. Some complex talents show additional information when hovered over or while holding ALT. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/talents@2332389]

## Unlocking talents

Talents have four tiers, unlocked at level 10/15/20/25 and indicated by a Link▶️ sound effect. When a tier unlocks, the player chooses one of its two talents; its effects apply immediately, while the other talent remains locked until later.

At level 27/28/29/30, the tiers unlock a second time, allowing the previously unchosen talent to be selected in addition to the earlier choice.

Talents are leveled independently of hero abilities and the Attribute Bonus and do not share points with them. A player can therefore level an ability or attribute points and choose a talent at the same level.

Illusions and clones copy the hero’s talent choices when they are created and do not update after the hero makes further choices. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/talents@2332389#Unlocking_Talents]
