# Strength

Strength is one of the three primary attributes. It grants every hero health and health regeneration, while strength heroes also gain attack damage per point of strength. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/strength@2381952]

## Bonuses

Every point of strength provides:

| Bonus | Value |
|---|---:|
| Maximum health | 22 |
| Health regeneration | 0.1 |
| Main attack damage for strength heroes | 1 |

[corpus:liquipedia_dota2/strength@2381952#Bonuses]

The health bonus applies only to the maximum health pool. Current health is not increased, but retains its current percentage. Gaining 1 health regeneration requires 10 strength points. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/strength@2381952#Bonuses]

## Strength Heroes

Strength heroes tend to have more health and health regeneration than other heroes, making them more resilient to physical attacks and spells. Their roles include carry, ganker, and support, but almost all have some form of strong initiation and are intended to act as tanks to some degree. Heroes with large amounts of strength may greatly increase the damage required to bring their team down. With a few exceptions, most strength heroes are melee. Unlike intelligence heroes’ typically strong early game and agility heroes’ late-game excellence, strength heroes often balance the two and remain relevant throughout the game. There are 36 strength heroes. The growth columns show how much each base value increases per hero level. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/strength@2381952#Strength_Heroes]
