# Sleep

Sleep is a rare status effect that completely disables affected units for its entire duration. A sleeping unit is either awakenable by damage or invulnerable. Invulnerable sleeping units cannot be awakened; sleep sources that grant invulnerability therefore make damage unable to wake them. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/sleep@2380210]

Sleeping units are never attacked automatically, regardless of auto-attack settings. Lane creeps, neutral creeps, and buildings never automatically attack them because these units always use auto-attack. Player-controlled units also do not automatically attack sleeping targets, but players can force an attack by selecting the target with **A** or **Left Click**. Taunt sources can still force units to attack sleeping targets. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/sleep@2380210]
