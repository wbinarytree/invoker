# Snowballing

## Overview

Snowballing is an unofficial term for gaining enough momentum to become much stronger relative to other heroes at that stage. A hero good at snowballing can, after gaining sufficient advantage—usually during the early or mid game—completely dominate the enemy team unless its composition can answer the threat. A snowballing team has a composition that lets it preserve and increase an acquired advantage. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/snowballing@2148434]

Snowballers are distinct from carries. Carries scale well with items and can become strong late in the game after acquiring them, whereas snowballers maintain and increase existing momentum. A carry may reverse a losing game by obtaining enough items or prolonging the game, but a snowballer is generally unable to do so while performing badly because they require an initial advantage. Without that advantage, a snowballer may fall behind the rest of the team in overall usefulness. Some heroes, including Luna, can be both carries and snowballers. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/snowballing@2148434]

## Nature of snowballing heroes

Snowballers are often niche heroes with a few superb strengths and severe weaknesses. They snowball when a strong starting advantage lets them cover those weaknesses sufficiently for their strengths to shine; without it, the same weaknesses can leave them behind their team. They may depend heavily on situational abilities or items, unconventional opposing lineups, jungling, or occupying a particular lane—usually mid—which creates obvious counters. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/snowballing@2148434#Nature_of_Snowballing_heroes]

### Pudge as an example

Pudge is widely regarded as the best example of a snowballer. His very high tankiness, extreme burst damage, and ability to gank let him assassinate and isolate enemy heroes, enabling him to dominate the opposing team. Flesh Heap further amplifies this by granting bonus Strength whenever a hero is killed in his vicinity. His high starting Strength and Strength gain, combined with Flesh Heap, make it very difficult for opponents to recover and deal with him after he gains the upper hand. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/snowballing@2148434#Nature_of_Snowballing_heroes]

Pudge is almost completely reliant on landing Meat Hook to perform his role as a nuker because he lacks an ability that attracts focus fire and thereby makes use of his tankiness. Rot and Dismember require melee range, Dismember is channeled, and Pudge has no mobility and a below-average movement speed of 285. He therefore needs to land Meat Hook frequently to bring an opponent into range of his other abilities. Rot also damages Pudge, making it harder to use effectively after a missed hook and allowing heroes including Bloodseeker, Necrophos, and Doom to exploit it. Rot makes him reliant on a somewhat early Hood of Defiance, which greatly reduces its self-damage. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/snowballing@2148434#Nature_of_Snowballing_heroes]

Despite somewhat expensive ability mana costs, Pudge has low starting Intelligence (15) and Intelligence gain (1.5), resulting in a very low mana pool. He consequently relies on Bottle or Arcane Boots to have enough mana for his abilities. This item dependence is compounded by poor farming: he has only last-hitting to help him farm, while his playstyle emphasizes roaming and ganking rather than remaining in a lane. Kills are therefore his main income source. Without initial early-game kills, he falls far behind and exhausts his entire mana pool after a single combo. Observer Wards also counter him heavily by helping the opposing team avoid incoming hooks and prevent him from obtaining needed runes. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/snowballing@2148434#Nature_of_Snowballing_heroes]

Pudge also relies on playing solo, almost always in mid, making his plan obvious and easy to counter. Juggernaut, Omniknight, and Bloodseeker can be placed mid against him. Alternatively, a melee carry such as Phantom Assassin can occupy mid to exploit Pudge’s weak lane control and the likelihood that, after obtaining his key items, he will spend more time ganking than harassing the carry. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/snowballing@2148434#Nature_of_Snowballing_heroes]
