# Creep Control Techniques

## Last-hitting

Last-hitting means that a hero—or a creep under that hero’s control—deals the final blow to a neutral creep, enemy lane creep, enemy hero, or building. The hero dealing the killing blow to an enemy unit receives gold and experience. The exact last-hitter and assist-gold awards are covered under Gold. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Last-hitting]

Last-hitting enemy lane creeps naturally pushes the lane because it adds another damage source against the enemy wave. This can be counteracted by attacking and denying allied lane creeps or by pulling. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Last-hitting]

Auto-attacking means letting a hero attack at will. It keeps friendly units alive and helps with pushing, but makes last-hitting much more difficult. In the late game, heroes often deal enough damage per second to auto-attack while still securing last hits. Auto-attacking kills enemy creeps faster and is useful when deliberately pushing, especially when killing towers. Early-game pushing toward an enemy tower is dangerous because it places the hero far from allied towers and makes enemy ganks easier. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Last-hitting]

## Denying

Denying means preventing enemy heroes from last-hitting a friendly unit by dealing the final blow to that unit yourself. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Denying]

| Allied unit | Deny threshold | Condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Creeps and siege units | 50% | — |
| Heroes | 25% | Only under certain debuffs |
| Towers | 10% | — |

Illusions and summoned units act as creeps for these purposes. A deny prevents the enemy from receiving any gold and a portion of the kill’s experience. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Denying]

## Attack Animations and Projectile Speeds

Shorter attack animations make last-hitting easier for all heroes. Faster projectile speeds make it easier for ranged heroes. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Attack_animations_and_projectile_speeds]

## Equilibrium

Avoid pushing too far; keeping lane creeps fighting approximately in the middle of the lane prevents moving too far from the safety of an allied tower. Enemy ranged creeps are squishy and provide easy gold, but attacking them helps push the enemy creepwave toward the allied tower. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Equilibrium]

Creep equilibrium is generally maintained by balancing last-hitting with denying. Situationally, it can also be adjusted by attacking allied creeps before the deny point or attacking enemy creeps to push. This is also called static farming. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Equilibrium]

Creep pulling can restore equilibrium to the normal meeting-point, but the entire creepwave must die in the jungle. Equilibrium remains situational: being pushed may be safer, but harder pushing is appropriate if the enemy lane can slowly chip away at the allied tower. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Equilibrium]

## Creep Blocking

Creep blocking shifts lane equilibrium toward the blocker’s side. The hero physically obstructs allied creeps so that they take longer to reach the middle of the lane. The slower creeps meet the opposing wave closer to the allied tower, making farming safer for the blocker and riskier for the enemy. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Creep_Blocking]

Creep blocking is generally most useful on the first creep wave at the start of the game. In the middle lane, it can allow a hero to last-hit from their own side of the river rather than inside it. This high ground provides vision of the opponent’s side of the river and lets ranged heroes avoid the 25% miss chance associated with last-hitting uphill. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Creep_Blocking]

In the “long” or “safe” lane, creeps sprint until about halfway between the Tier 1 and 2 towers, making it much harder to block them all the way from the barracks. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Creep_Blocking]

## Pushing

Pushing means damaging enemy creeps with auto-attacks or abilities. It moves the creepwave toward the enemy tower or simply away from allied towers. In the late game, pushing moves creepwaves toward the enemy base to win the game. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Pushing]

The following abilities facilitate expedient pushing and can damage multiple creeps and towers at the same time:

| Hero | Ability |
|---|---|
| Leshrac | Diabolic Edict |
| Techies | Proximity Mines |
| Pugna | Nether Blast |

[corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Pushing]

## Pulling

Pulling redirects allied lane creeps toward a neutral camp, usually the easy or hard camp because those camps are closest to the lane. Assuming it does not create a double wave, pulling resets creep equilibrium, denies XP and gold to lane opponents, and grants additional XP and gold. It is not recommended when enemy creeps are about to push because this will hurt the tower or the carry tanking the creeps more. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Pulling]

Stacking the easy camp before pulling is a good habit because only 1 stack is usually insufficient to clear the creep wave. The resulting “double wave” will eventually reach the enemy tower. To prevent this, the wave can be repulled from the easy camp toward a medium or hard camp when the neutral creeps are about to die. This technique is called connecting the pull, double pulling, or chain pulling. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Pulling]

A support usually performs the pull while the carry stays in lane to farm lane creeps, which provide more gold and experience. This gives the support a good amount of gold and experience without leeching it from the carry. The drawback is that the carry remains alone in lane and becomes more vulnerable to all-ins from enemy heroes. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/creep_control_techniques@2079246#Pulling]

## Roles and Last-hitting

| Role | Priorities |
|---|---|
| Carry | Focus most attention on securing last hits. |
| Support | Deny creeps and harass enemy heroes so the carry can last-hit without being harassed. Do not abandon last-hitting entirely: some creeps may be killed faster than the carry can enter attack range. Supports can also stack creeps to help the carry farm. |
