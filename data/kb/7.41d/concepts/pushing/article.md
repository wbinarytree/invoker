# Pushing

Pushing is the process of clearing enemy creep waves and building large allied creep waves to destroy buildings rapidly. Chen and Shadow Shaman are depicted destroying a Tower. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pushing@2148417]

## Advantages

Pushing is ultimately necessary to win. Choosing when to push is crucial to team strategy because some lineups peak in the late game, while others can finish the game very early. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pushing@2148417#The_advantages_of_pushing]

Early pushes pressure the enemy team by forcing it to defend its towers. This counters carry heroes, which need time and farm to become effective. Destroying towers also earns gold for the entire team because every hero gains bounty. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pushing@2148417#The_advantages_of_pushing]

Pushing counters turtling by making it difficult for enemies to farm while their towers and creep waves remain under threat. It forces them to defend, usually resulting in an impending team clash. Most heroes push when the Tower life is low, and pushing toward the enemy can sometimes lead to clashes or teamfights. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pushing@2148417#The_advantages_of_pushing]

## Means of Pushing

### Heroes

Summoning abilities such as Nature's Prophet's **Nature's Call** are excellent for pushing: their summons can attack enemy creeps and buildings while absorbing large amounts of damage. Spammable area-of-effect nukes such as Death Prophet's **Crypt Swarm** quickly eliminate hostile creep waves and maintain a push. Area-of-effect healing abilities such as Necrophos's **Death Pulse** greatly improve allied-creep durability and sustain a push. Abilities including Leshrac's **Diabolic Edict** and Pugna's **Nether Blast** can directly damage towers. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pushing@2148417#Heroes]

### Items

Most pushing items buff or heal allied units, letting creeps inflict more damage and survive longer. They also help heroes remain away from their fountain for extended periods and maintain the push. Arcane Boots and heals can make long fountain trips obsolete, allowing the entire team to remain outside and gank or push. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pushing@2148417#Items]

During later stages, carries can push lanes quickly by themselves. Multi-unit items such as Battle Fury or Maelstrom further improve this capability. When the enemy team is dead or far from its base, Boots of Travel can quickly teleport its user to a vulnerable lane. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pushing@2148417#Items]

## Countering Pushes

Enemy pushes can be countered in several ways, most resembling pushing methods themselves. Counter-pushing can begin with one team ganker attacking an enemy hero while defending the tower. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pushing@2148417#Countering_pushes]

Ganking is the best counter to a push because advancing enemies become increasingly exposed as they approach the base, making ganks easier to execute. However, ganking is usually not an anti-push measure that prevents a push from occurring; it is a counter after the push begins. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pushing@2148417#Countering_pushes]

Long-range area-of-effect abilities such as Keeper of the Light's **Illuminate** and Windranger's **Powershot** can devastate enemy creep waves safely and substantially slow a push. Summoned units such as enchanted neutral creeps are less effective defensively but can absorb damage. Venomancer's **Plague Ward** is the most effective defensive summon because the wards are spell immune, inflict enhanced damage against creeps, and can be summoned in large amounts with low mana cost and cooldown. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pushing@2148417#Countering_pushes]

Initiation is the most important part of starting a battle. Because pushing teams are often clustered and stationary, initiating against them is especially easy. Earthshaker's **Echo Slam** combined with a **Blink Dagger** can devastate numerous clustered opponents, while other initiators or tanky heroes can also make the first move. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pushing@2148417#Countering_pushes]

Against a pushing lineup, teams should remain together and avoid dying individually because the enemy can quickly convert a small advantage into tower or barracks kills. Defensive **Observer Wards** and moving with all five players can prevent dangerous situations. Because heroes such as Nature's Prophet often try to push unprotected lanes, always carrying a **Town Portal Scroll** is extremely important for defending incoming pushes. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/pushing@2148417#Countering_pushes]
