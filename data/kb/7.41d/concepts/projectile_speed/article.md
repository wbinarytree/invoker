# Projectile Speed

## Definition

Projectile Speed is the speed at which a unit’s attack projectile travels. A projectile speed of 900 means that the projectile travels 900 units per second. The base projectile speed is 900. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/projectile_speed@2243749]

Every unit currently has a base attack projectile speed of 900. Melee heroes have their projectile speed set to 900 if they should ever need it. Certain projectile-based abilities also have projectile speed; Magic Missile is listed as an example. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/projectile_speed@2243749#Definition]

## Spell Reflection

Reflected abilities act as though the reflecting hero casts them back at enemies, with an instant cast time and without being interrupted. Their scaling values use the reflecting hero’s stats rather than the original caster’s stats. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/projectile_speed@2243749#Spell_Reflection]

## Instant Attacks

Projectile-based instant attack sources are usually reflected if the affected target has a projectile speed upon impact. Heroes with a projectile speed of 0 do not reflect projectile-based instant attack sources. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/projectile_speed@2243749#Instant_Attack]
