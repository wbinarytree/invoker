# Ganking

Ganking is the act of actively moving around the map to kill an enemy hero. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/ganking@2148370]

## Advantages of ganking

Ganking provides an overall experience and gold advantage. Successful ganks can significantly slow an enemy carry’s farm, help a teammate recover a difficult lane, and create a window to push down a tower or kill Roshan. Active ganking and pushing are essentially mandatory to gain an early advantage. When the opposing team has a large late-game advantage, ganking is often the easiest way to kill its heroes. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/ganking@2148370#The_advantages_of_ganking]

Ganking also counters pushes: enemy pushers must move deep into your territory, where they are often isolated and have little vision. After ganking them, your team can counterpush to negate the advantage they were attempting to gain. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/ganking@2148370#The_advantages_of_ganking]

## Means of ganking

### Heroes

Most ganking heroes can slow or stun enemies, making kills easier to set up for allies. Two distinguishable roles gank frequently: gankers and roamers. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/ganking@2148370#Heroes]

#### Gankers

Gankers are extremely efficient at ganking and often destroy opponents in one-on-one combat through surprise. Some depend primarily on ganking for farm and must secure kills in other lanes to buy items. Most require quick experience and scale best with early levels. Their movement around the map also makes them effective rune carriers, especially with a Bottle, because it gives them opportunities to use offensive runes. #### Roamers

Roamers focus on warding and early-game map control, often scouting territory and spending little time in lanes. Because they do not need to remain in a lane, they frequently assist ganks, especially when equipped with a powerful disable. Most can gank before reaching level 6, possibly excepting Bane, Jakiro, and some others. Zeus, Lina, Leshrac, Spirit Breaker, and Lion are powerful gankers that may make ganking their primary role. ### Items

Both item-dependent and item-independent gankers can use items to ease their work. Blink Dagger, Force Staff, Phase Boots and, to some extent, Tranquil Boots, Town Portal Scrolls, and Boots of Travel help players enter battle faster and surprise opposing heroes. Shadow Blade and Smoke of Deceit provide invisibility. Smoke of Deceit is especially useful because it is cheap and tremendously increases movement speed, although the base store’s stock is limited to three. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/ganking@2148370#Items]

Successful gankers rarely return to base, so Arcane Boots, Bottles, or Clarity Potions are needed to preserve health and mana. Hit-point regeneration items are also helpful, especially Urn of Shadows and Magic Wand. Additional disables are not necessary, but Eul’s Scepter of Divinity and Rod of Atos are useful in the lategame. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/ganking@2148370#Items]

## Preventing enemy ganks

### Positioning and towers

Turtling efficiently avoids ganks by farming away from enemy territory and preventing creeps from pushing too far through last-hitting enemy creeps and denying. During the early game, ganking a hero beside their tower is difficult: the tower deals high damage, and other enemies can use Town Portal Scrolls to reach it in time and kill the ganker. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/ganking@2148370#Preventing_enemy_ganks]

### Vision and reinforcement

A team should maintain vision over key map areas with Observer Wards, while using Sentry Wards to reveal invisible heroes and enemy wards. Town Portal Scrolls can assist allies being ganked on the other side of the map. Carrying Town Portal Scrolls, maintaining vision, and watching the minimap can let a team quickly gain a numbers advantage over the gankers and kill them. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/ganking@2148370#Preventing_enemy_ganks]

### Missing calls

Teammates can be protected by reporting when an enemy leaves a lane. In public games this is done through team chat; for example, calling “missing” warns that enemy hero(es) have left a lane and may be ganking. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/ganking@2148370#Preventing_enemy_ganks]

### Escape mechanisms

Abilities such as Mirana’s Leap are well suited to escaping incoming ganks and are often called escape mechanisms. Heroes without one, such as Shadow Fiend and Silencer, are susceptible to ganks and must be especially careful when enemy heroes are missing from the minimap. Such heroes can purchase items such as Force Staff to gain an escape mechanism. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/ganking@2148370#Preventing_enemy_ganks]

### Map-control abilities

Some heroes provide map control through temporary vision, pseudo-wards, temporary summons, and illusions. Clockwerk’s Rocket Flare, Vengeful Spirit’s Wave of Terror, and most of Zeus’ abilities reveal the map temporarily for an advantage. Venomancer’s Plague Ward is a strong anti-gank tool because its wards provide vision and can be spammed. Minions summoned by Lycan’s Summon Wolves, Enchantress’ Enchant, and Nature’s Prophet’s Nature’s Call also provide vision. Slardar’s Corrosive Haze, Bounty Hunter’s Track, and Necronomicon have some anti-gank utility. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/ganking@2148370#Preventing_enemy_ganks]
