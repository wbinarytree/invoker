# Aura

Auras are passive abilities that grant buffs or debuffs to units within an area around the holder or, in some cases, for a short period at a targeted point or unit. Different auras have different linger durations, continuing to affect units briefly after they leave the effect radius. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/aura@2395696]

## Definition

Aura buffs and debuffs are generally not dispellable. Multiple auras of the same kind or sourced from the same base item generally do not stack, although both rules have exceptions.

Most auras cannot be controlled and always affect units around their holder, while some can be toggled. Some have an active component that can temporarily boost the aura or determine whether it affects only surrounding heroes. Active components and toggles are treated as regular abilities and cannot be used or toggled while silenced or disabled. Temporary auras can also be provided for a set period by casting an ability.

Each aura determines which units it affects; units that do not meet its criteria are unaffected. Auras may affect only ranged units, melee units, heroes, or non-heroes. As a rule of thumb, positive auras affect allied units, including the holder, while negative auras affect enemies, but exceptions exist.

Auras usually affect invisible units and units in the Fog of War and can be bestowed by them, which can often reveal that an invisible enemy is nearby. Almost all auras do not affect invulnerable or hidden units. Illusions can bestow most passive auras but usually do not benefit from them. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/aura@2395696#Definition]

## Positive Aura Sources

Positive auras provide buffs or bonuses to the holder and their team. They generally stack or synergise with abilities from the caster and their team. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/aura@2395696#Positive_Aura_Sources]
