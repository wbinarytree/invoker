# Leash

Leash is a status effect that prevents affected units from casting certain mobility spells, using the same ability list as root. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/leash@2363699]

## Mechanics

Leash disables the casting of several mobility abilities, including Blink-based abilities, and shares several interactions with root. Applying leash does not cancel an ability that has already been cast and does not interrupt channeling. However, it always cancels teleports from Town Portal Scroll, Keen Conveyance, and Return. [corpus:liquipedia_dota2/leash@2363699#Mechanics]
